How to use this

  1. Type what they're buying.
  2. Enter the cost of one in the United States (or tap a chip).
  3. Pick how many each gets.
  4. Add each recipient: tap the country, add the name / charity.
  5. Each row shows what to send; type a real local price if you know it.
  6. Total is at the bottom; tap Copy plan to share.

This plans the amounts. It does not send money.

About the numbers. Price levels are from the World Bank (household consumption price level, mostly 2024; US = 100, stored as 1.0). Exchange rates are live today. A country's official cost = US cost × its 2024 household PPP, turned into dollars at today's rate. Official prices are cost of living, not one grocery item and not today's wire rate.
